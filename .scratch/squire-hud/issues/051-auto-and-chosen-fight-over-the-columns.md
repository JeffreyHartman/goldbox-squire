# 051 — `auto` and `chosen` fight over the columns

Type: `wayfinder:grilling`
Status: open
Triage: `needs-triage`
Blocked by: none

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## What is wrong

The `c` key and the layout rule both decide how many cards go across, and the
status line reports one number for both of them. Jeff noticed them fighting in
a real window. Three collisions are in the code today.

**The first press of `c` jumps backwards.** `View::cycle_columns` starts from
`even.first()`, which is always one card across, rather than from the number on
screen. A six-strong party in a wide window shows three across. Press `c` and it
goes to one, not to six.

**An ask that does not fit is held but never shown.** `layout::grid` drops an
unfittable ask and returns the rule's answer, which is right. The ask stays in
`Toggles::across`, and the status line says `auto`, so nothing on screen holds
the number the next `c` will step from. Press `c` again and the count skips.

**`chosen` does not mean the user chose.** The status line asks whether the ask
equals what is drawn, not whether an ask exists. Drag a window until a chosen
three no longer fits and the word flips to `auto` with no key pressed. It flips
back on the way out.

## What has to be decided

The real question is what the key means, and the three collisions all follow
from it. Two shapes, and they are not compatible:

- **The key sets a preference the rule may overrule.** What it is now. The
  status has to say both numbers, or say that an ask is being held and not
  honoured.
- **The key sets the arrangement, and one that does not fit is not offered.**
  `cycle_columns` walks only the arrangements that fit the window, so an ask
  is always what is drawn and one word is honest. A resize can then invalidate
  the ask, and what happens to it needs an answer.

Also open: whether `c` should start from what is drawn (it should), and whether
there is a key back to `auto` other than cycling off the end.

## Where it lives

- The rule and the key were specified apart, in `036-layout-plan.md` and
